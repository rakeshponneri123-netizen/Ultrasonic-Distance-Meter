========================================================================
HARDWARE PACKAGING & VISUAL UI DISPLAY SPECIFICATIONS
========================================================================

1. PRODUCT PHYSICAL STRUCTURE & LAYOUT:
- Front Edge: Features twin acoustic cylinders resembling eyes (one Transmitter nozzle and one Receiver nozzle).
- Top Surface: Integrated digital OLED or 7-segment character display.
- Rear Module: Houses a small piezo buzzer component utilized for proximity sound triggers.

2. SYSTEM VISUAL DISPLAY READOUT MATRIX:

[NOMINAL STATE READING]
--------------------------------
| DISTANCE: 150 CM |  <- Row 1 (Displays calculated numeric range)
| ZONE: SAFE       |  <- Row 2 (Confirms obstacle is far away)
--------------------------------

[CRITICAL PROXIMITY ALERT SCREEN]
--------------------------------
| DISTANCE: 10 CM  |  <- Row 1 (Flashes numeric metrics rapidly)
| ALERT: TOO CLOSE |  <- Row 2 (Displays critical trigger text)
--------------------------------

3. PROXIMITY ALARM FREQUENCY MATRIX:
- Object Distance > 100 cm: Buzzer state remains completely silent.
- Object Distance between 20 cm and 50 cm: Buzzer gives slow warning pulses.
- Object Distance < 15 cm: Buzzer activates a continuous high-frequency tone.
